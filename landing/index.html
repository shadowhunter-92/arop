Integration

One line of code. Zero disruption.

AROP sits in front of any AI provider. Your existing code doesn't change — just the URL it points to.

1

Deploy AROP (10 minutes)

Run AROP on your own server with Docker, or use our cloud. Either way, your data stays under your control.

docker compose up -d # that's it
2

Change one line in your app

Point your OpenAI SDK at AROP instead of OpenAI. Works with any language, any framework, any AI provider.

# Before
base_url = "https://api.openai.com/v1"

# After
base_url = "https://your-arop-server/v1"
3

Everything is logged and governed automatically

Every AI call flows through AROP. Guardrails fire. Costs are tracked. Audit logs are written. Your compliance team has what they need.
